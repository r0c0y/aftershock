Meridian Marketplace Agreement, version 1 (early term sheet draft).
Larkspur Supply will list its Pro and Team plans on the Meridian Cloud Marketplace.
Meridian charges a marketplace commission of 7% of gross merchandise value (GMV) on every sale through the listing.
This version was replaced by v2 after Meridian asked for a different payout schedule. Do not rely on it.
