EXECUTED - signed by both parties on 22 September 2026. Meridian Marketplace Agreement, final.
Section 4, Fees: Meridian shall retain a marketplace fee of twelve percent (12%) of GMV on all sales made through the
Meridian listing, effective 1 September 2026. The earlier proposal of nine percent (9%) in prior drafts is withdrawn.
Payout is net 30. The fee is fixed for 12 months from the effective date.
